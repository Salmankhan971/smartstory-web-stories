7 Best Free AI Tools for Students and Teachers

A practical, non-ranked starting point for studying, research, visuals, and lesson planning.

Photo: Unsplash

01 · Flexible study partner

ChatGPT

Explain a hard concept, make practice questions, analyze an approved file, or draft a study guide. Teachers can turn classroom material into a worksheet draft.

Verify facts and citations. Free tools have separate, changeable limits; protect sensitive student data.

Official free-tier details

02 · Multimodal learning

Google Gemini

Transform notes, unpack a difficult passage, brainstorm a lesson explanation, or plan a revision session.

Free limits vary with feature, demand, account, region, and plan. Review privacy settings and school controls.

Explore Gemini · Limits

03 · Your sources, clearer study

NotebookLM

Upload permitted notes or PDFs to make source-grounded Q&A, flashcards, quizzes, reports, or audio study overviews.

Citations help you trace an answer, but still check the original. Upload only material you have rights to use.

Official site · Availability and limits

04 · Make ideas visual

Canva AI

Create editable presentations, infographics, worksheets, quizzes, and classroom activities from a prompt.

Free access is allowance-based and varies by tool. Review facts, image rights, trademarks, and library licenses.

Canva AI · AI access

05 · Research with links

Perplexity

Search the web in real time, inspect citations, and use Learn Mode for hints, flashcards, or guided practice.

Basic searches are described as practically unlimited, but other free features are limited. Open and verify every original source.

How it works · Learn Mode

06–07 · Two more options

Copilot + Khanmigo

Copilot: useful for everyday brainstorming, explanations, and presentation outlines. Account and school settings vary.

Khanmigo: a guided-learning option for working through problems and exploring teacher-support workflows where available.

Check current age, country, plan, and school eligibility before recommending either tool.

Microsoft Copilot · Khanmigo

Use AI responsibly

Draft, check, protect

Use AI as a coach—not an answer key. Verify claims, citations, calculations, and images. Keep private student data out unless authorized. Respect copyright and academic-integrity rules.
